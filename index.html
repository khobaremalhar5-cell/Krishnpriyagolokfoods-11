About KrishnaPriya Golok Foods

Based in the sacred land of Arli BK, Taluka Tuljapur, Jilha Dharashiv, near Solapur City, KrishnaPriya Golok Foods has been built on values of purity, transparency, and tradition. We are the trusted source of authentic, untouched, and naturally processed grains and flour products.

Our founder, Sushilkumar Khobare, envisioned a model that bypasses corporate middlemen to link pure soil directly with your kitchen. By ensuring 100% natural processing with zero additives, we restore the true nutrition of grains to your family's table.
