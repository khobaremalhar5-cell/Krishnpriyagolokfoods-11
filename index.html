About KrishnaPriya Golok Foods

Based in the sacred land of Arli BK, Taluka Tuljapur, Jilha Dharashiv, near Solapur City, KrishnaPriya Golok Foods has been built on values of purity, transparency, and health.

Our founder, Sushilkumar Khobare, envisioned a model that bypasses corporate middlemen to link pure soil directly with your kitchen. By ensuring 100% natural processing, we preserve the vital dietary fiber and vitamins that commercial fast-milling completely strips away.
